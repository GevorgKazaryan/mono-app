MonoPrivacy

Privacy

Effective 24 September 2026

Mono collects no data about you. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

What Mono stores

Mono keeps the following only on your device:

  • Your ink-hours schedule (start and end times).
  • The apps, categories and websites you chose to quiet. iOS stores these as opaque tokens. Mono can’t see their names and doesn’t know what they are.
  • Whatever you write on the Night page.
  • A count of the quiet nights you’ve completed, shown in the app and its widgets.

If you delete Mono, all of this is deleted with it.

Screen Time

Mono uses Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) framework only to rest the apps you choose during the hours you choose. Apple designed this framework so apps like Mono never learn which apps you use or how long you use them. Mono doesn’t read your Screen Time usage reports.

What Mono does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No network requests. Mono has no servers.
  • No sharing or selling of data. There is no data to share or sell.

Children

Mono collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
